# Polls

A poll is a post with a question and a set of choices that people can vote on.

## Creating a poll

- **On the web:** in the compose box, click the **Poll** button in the toolbar.
- **In the mobile app:** in the compose box, tap the bar chart button in the toolbar. The text box changes to **Ask a question…**

Type your question as the post text, then fill in the options:

- A poll needs at least **2 options** and can have up to **6**. Use **Add option** to add more, and the remove button beside an option to take it away.
- Each option can be up to **100 characters**.

Under the options are the poll's settings:

- **Duration:** how long the poll stays open. The default is **1 day**.
  - On the web you can choose **1 hour**, **6 hours**, **12 hours**, **1 day**, **3 days**, **1 week** or **No limit**.
  - In the mobile app you can choose **1h**, **1d**, **3d**, **1w** or **∞** (no limit).
- **Allow multiple selections:** people can vote for more than one option. Off by default.
- **Let respondents add options:** people can add their own option to the poll. Off by default.

Choose who can see the poll with the visibility selector, the same as any other post (see [Making a post](posting.md)), and post it. In the mobile app, a poll can't have photos or a video attached.

## Voting

Tap or click an option to vote. Once you've voted, the poll shows the percentage of votes for each option, with your choice marked with a ✓. Until you vote, the results stay hidden.

- **Single choice poll:** voting for a different option moves your vote there.
- **Multiple choice poll:** the poll says **Select all that apply**, and each option you tap is added to your votes.
- **Removing your vote:** on the web, click **Remove vote** under the poll. In either app, tapping an option you chose removes your vote.
- **Adding an option:** if the poll lets respondents add options, choose **Add your own option…**, type it, and add it. Your vote goes to the option you added. A poll can grow to at most 10 options this way.

While a poll is open, it shows when it **Closes**. When the time is up, it shows **This poll has ended**, the final results are shown to everyone, and no more votes or new options can be added.

People who aren't signed in can see a public poll's results but can't vote.

## Seeing who voted

Votes aren't anonymous. Click or tap the vote count under a poll (for example, **12 votes**) to open **Poll votes**, which lists who voted for each option. Anyone who can see the poll can see this list.

## Polls on the Fediverse and Bluesky

A public poll is shared with the wider network along with the rest of your public posts:

- **Mastodon and other [Fediverse](fediverse.md) servers** get a real poll that people there can vote in. Their votes are counted in your poll.
- **[Bluesky](bluesky.md)** has no polls, so your poll appears there as text: your question, the options as a list, and a **Vote:** link back to the poll on Agora. If your account federates to Bluesky, the mobile app reminds you of this while you're writing the poll.

You can also come across polls from Fediverse accounts you follow:

- **In the mobile app** you can vote in them. Your vote is sent to the server the poll came from, which can take a moment.
- **On the web** they're marked as results from the Fediverse. You can see the results, but you vote on the original post on its own server.
