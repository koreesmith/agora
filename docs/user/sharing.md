# Sharing a Post

Sharing (also called reposting) puts someone else's post in front of your own audience, with or without something of your own to say about it.

## What can be shared

Only **public** posts can be shared. A post shared only with friends or a friend list stays with the audience its author chose:

- On the web, the share button on those posts is greyed out (**Friends-only posts cannot be shared**).
- In the mobile app, tapping it shows **Cannot share**.

Posts in a [group](groups.md) can't be shared outside the group.

## Sharing a post

1. Click or tap the share button (two arrows in a loop) under the post.
2. In **Share post**, add your own comment if you want (**Say something about this… (optional)** on the web). The post you're sharing is shown below it.
3. Choose who can see your share:
   - **Public**: anyone
   - **Friends**: only your friends (the default)
   - **Friend List**: only the people on one of your [friend lists](friend-lists.md). Pick the list from the menu that appears.
4. To put a content warning on your own comment, turn on **TW** and describe it, the same as on a post (see [Making a post](posting.md)).
5. Click or tap **Share**.

**Shared!** appears briefly when it's done. The share button turns green on the web, or your theme colour in the mobile app, on posts you've shared.

Your audience choice only covers your share. It doesn't change who can see the original post, which stays public.

## How a share appears

Your share shows up in your followers' feeds and on your profile marked **Reposted**, with your comment (if you wrote one) above the original post. Click or tap the original to open it, with all of its own comments and reactions.

The post's author is notified that you shared it.

If you share a post that is itself a share, you share the original post.

## Sharing posts from Mastodon and Bluesky

You can share public posts from accounts you follow on [the Fediverse](fediverse.md) and [Bluesky](bluesky.md):

- The share is sent to their network as a boost (on Mastodon and other Fediverse servers) or a repost (on Bluesky), so the author sees it there too.
- If you added a comment to a public share, your comment also goes out to the Fediverse as a post of its own, with a link to the post you shared. This happens whoever wrote the original.
