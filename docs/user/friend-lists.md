# Friend Lists

A friend list is a named group of people, like "Close friends" or "Work". You can use a list to share a post with just those people, or to read just their posts. Lists are private: nobody is told when you add them to a list, and nobody else can see your lists.

## Creating a list

- **On the web:** go to **Connections** and open the **Friend Lists** tab. Type a name in **New list name (e.g. Close Friends, Work…)** and press Enter or click **+**.
- **In the mobile app:** go to **Settings → Friend lists**, type a name in **List name…**, and add it.

## Adding and removing people

You can add your friends to a list. You can also add accounts you follow on [the Fediverse](fediverse.md) or [Bluesky](bluesky.md), so one list can mix people from all three networks.

**From the list itself:** open the list (click or tap it) to see its **Members**, with everyone you can still add under **Add people**. Use the add button beside a person to add them, or the remove button beside a member to take them off the list.

**From a person:** the **Add to friend lists** picker lets you choose every list a person belongs on at once. Select the lists, then save. Choose **Skip** to close it without changing anything. The picker opens:

- when you tap or click the **List** icon beside an account on the **Connections** page's **Fediverse** or **Bluesky** tab (web and mobile app)
- on the web, right after you send someone a friend request or accept one, so you can sort a new friend straight away

## Sharing a post with a list

When you write a post, set its visibility to **Friend List** and choose the list. Only the people on that list (and you) can see the post. See [Making a post](posting.md).

You can do the same when you [share a post](sharing.md), to share it with just one list.

Who can see a list post follows the list as it is now, not as it was when you posted:

- Add someone to the list and they can see your earlier posts to that list too.
- Remove someone and they can no longer see them.
- Delete the list and its posts are visible only to you.

## Reading a list's posts

Each list has its own feed of posts from just the people on it:

- **On the web:** click the list's name on the **Friend Lists** tab.
- **In the mobile app:** tap **View feed** on the list.

For a feed that combines a list with other filters, go to **My Feeds** and create a custom feed with a friend list filter.

## Deleting a list

- **On the web:** use the delete button on the list and confirm.
- **In the mobile app:** use the delete button on the list and confirm **Delete**.

Deleting a list doesn't unfriend or unfollow anyone on it.
