# Reposting and Sharing

There are two ways to pass a post along:

- **Repost** puts someone else's post in front of your own audience on Agora, with or without something of your own to say about it.
- **Share** sends a link to the post somewhere else: a message, an email, Bluesky, Mastodon, or anywhere you can paste a link.

## What can be reposted or shared

Only **public** posts can be reposted or shared. A post shared only with friends or a friend list stays with the audience its author chose:

- On the web, the repost button on those posts is greyed out (**Friends-only posts cannot be reposted**) and there is no share button.
- In the mobile app, tapping repost shows **Cannot repost**, and there is no share button.

Posts in a [group](groups.md) can't be reposted or shared outside the group.

## Reposting a post

1. Click or tap the repost button (two arrows in a loop) under the post.
2. In **Repost**, add your own comment if you want (**Say something about this… (optional)** on the web). The post you're reposting is shown below it.
3. Choose who can see your repost:
   - **Public**: anyone
   - **Friends**: only your friends (the default)
   - **Friend List**: only the people on one of your [friend lists](friend-lists.md). Pick the list from the menu that appears.
4. To put a content warning on your own comment, turn on **TW** and describe it, the same as on a post (see [Making a post](posting.md)).
5. Click or tap **Repost**.

**Reposted!** appears briefly when it's done. The repost button turns green on the web, or your theme colour in the mobile app, on posts you've reposted.

Your audience choice only covers your repost. It doesn't change who can see the original post, which stays public.

### How a repost appears

Your repost shows up in your followers' feeds and on your profile marked **Reposted**, with your comment (if you wrote one) above the original post. Click or tap the original to open it, with all of its own comments and reactions.

The post's author is notified that you reposted it.

If you repost a post that is itself a repost, you repost the original post.

### Reposting posts from Mastodon and Bluesky

You can repost public posts from accounts you follow on [the Fediverse](fediverse.md) and [Bluesky](bluesky.md):

- The repost is sent to their network as a boost (on Mastodon and other Fediverse servers) or a repost (on Bluesky), so the author sees it there too.
- If you added a comment to a public repost, your comment also goes out to the Fediverse as a post of its own, with a link to the post you reposted. This happens whoever wrote the original.

## Sharing a link to a post

Click the share button (a box with an arrow) under the post. You don't need to be signed in to share a public post.

On the web, the menu offers:

- **Copy link**: copies the link so you can paste it anywhere. **Link copied** confirms it.
- **Email**: opens a new email in your mail app with the link in it.
- **Post to Bluesky**: opens Bluesky's composer with the link filled in.
- **Post to Mastodon**: asks which Mastodon or Fediverse server you use the first time (for example `mastodon.social`), then opens that server's composer with the link filled in. Your browser remembers the server; choose **Use a different server** to change it.
- **More options…**: your device's own share sheet, in browsers that have one.

In the mobile app, the share button opens your phone's share sheet.

### Which link is shared

- A post written on Agora shares its Agora link. Anyone can open it, signed in or not.
- A post from the Fediverse or Bluesky shares the original post on its own network, so the person you send it to sees it where it was written.

When an Agora link is pasted into a message or another app, it shows a preview with the author's name, the start of the post, and its first image. A post with a content warning shows only the warning, and no image.
