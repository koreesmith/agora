# Using More Than One Account (Mobile App)

The Agora mobile app can stay signed in to up to **5 accounts** at once and switch between them in a tap. The accounts can be on the same Agora instance or on different ones, for example a personal account and a work account, or accounts on two different instances.

This is a mobile app feature. On the web, you're signed in to one account per browser.

## Adding an account

1. Open the account switcher: on the **Profile** tab, tap the **people icon** at the top. You can also press and hold the **Profile** tab itself.
2. Tap **Add account**.
3. Choose the instance and sign in as usual.

The new account becomes the one you're using. If you're already signed in to 5 accounts, sign out of one first.

## Switching accounts

Open the account switcher and tap the account you want. The app reloads your feed, notifications and messages for that account.

A dot on the people icon means you're signed in to more than one account. When you have more than one, composers in groups and on walls show a **Posting as @name** line, so you can see which account a post will come from before you send it.

## Notifications

Push notifications come from the account you're currently using. When you switch, the app moves notifications over to the new account, so you aren't notified for the others until you switch back to them. Each account's unread notifications are still waiting for you when you do.

## Signing out

Go to **Settings → Accounts** (or tap **Manage accounts** in the account switcher). From there you can:

- **Sign out** of one account. The others stay signed in.
- **Add account**.
- **Sign out of all accounts**. You'll need to sign back in to each one afterwards.
