# Reels

Reels are short videos, shown one at a time in a full-screen feed. Swipe or scroll to go to the next one.

A reel is an ordinary post with a video. It shows up in your friends' regular feeds as well, it appears on your profile, and it reaches the Fediverse and Bluesky the same way any video post does.

## Watching

- **On the web:** choose **Reels** in the sidebar.
- **In the mobile app:** tap **Reels** in the bottom bar. If you've moved it into **More**, open it from there (see [Customizing the bottom bar](#customizing-the-bottom-bar)).

There are two tabs at the top:

- **Following** shows videos from you, your friends, pages you follow, and the Fediverse and Bluesky accounts you follow. The newest come first.
- **Discover** shows public videos from this instance and the instances it's connected to. It doesn't show your own videos, and it doesn't include people on the wider Fediverse or Bluesky unless you follow them (those appear in Following). An admin can turn Discover off, in which case you'll only see Following.

Only the video on screen plays. When you move on, the one you left goes back to the start.

| | Web | Mobile app |
|---|---|---|
| Next or previous | Scroll, swipe, the arrow keys, or **j** and **k** | Swipe up or down |
| Pause or play | Click the video, or press the space bar | Tap the video |
| Like | The like button | The like button, or double-tap the video |
| Sound | The speaker button, or press **m** | The speaker button |

Sound starts off on the web, because browsers don't allow videos to play with sound until you've interacted with the page, and on in the app. Whichever you choose stays for the rest of your visit.

Each reel has the same actions as a post: react, open the comments, repost, bookmark and share. The **...** menu lets you **hide** the post, **report** it, or **block** the person.

### Videos you've already watched

A reel counts as watched once it has played for a few seconds. Watched reels don't come back, so the feed doesn't show you the same clips every time. When you reach the end it says **You're all caught up**. Choose **Watch older videos** to see the ones you've already watched.

### Content warnings

If a reel has a content warning, the video stays hidden behind a **Show reel** button. It doesn't count as watched until you show it.

## Making a reel

Choose the **+** button at the top right of the Reels page or screen.

1. **Pick a video.** Choose a file, or record one with your camera. Recording stops by itself at the length limit.
2. **Trim it and pick a cover.** Drag the start and end handles to keep only the part you want. Then choose the cover, which is the frame people see before the video plays.
3. **Add details.** Write a caption, add a content warning if you want one, choose who can see it, and, if it's public, which networks it goes to. These work the same way as they do on a normal post (see [Making a post](posting.md)).

Reels are up to **2 minutes**, unless the instance's admin has set a shorter limit. A longer video can still be used: it starts with its first stretch selected, and you move the handles to choose the part to keep.

After you post, the video is processed in the background. On the web, keep the box open until it finishes. In the mobile app you can leave the screen: progress shows on the Reels screen, and a message tells you when your reel is posted or if something went wrong, with a **Retry** button.

If your browser can't preview a file, you can't trim it there, but you can still post it as it is if it's within the length limit.

### Video storage

An admin can limit how much video storage each account may use. If you reach it, you'll be asked to delete one of your videos before uploading another.

## Customizing the bottom bar

In the mobile app the bottom bar holds Feed, Profile and up to three other places, plus **More**. Press and hold any tab except Profile to choose which places stay in the bar. Anything you leave out is in **More**. You can also do this from **More** or from **Settings**.

## Reporting a reel

Use the **...** menu on a reel and choose **Report**. Moderators can play the reported video from the report, and an admin or moderator who removes someone else's video takes the file off the server.
