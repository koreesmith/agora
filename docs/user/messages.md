# Direct Messages

Direct messages are one-to-one conversations that only you and the other person can see in the app.

## Starting a conversation

- **On the web:** go to **Messages** and click the **+** (New message) button. Search your friends or type a username, add an optional first message, and start the conversation.
- **From a profile:** on a friend's profile, click **Message**.
- **In the mobile app:** open the **Messages** tab and tap the compose button at the top.

If you already have a conversation with that person, you're taken to it rather than starting a second one.

You can send text and photos, and react to a message with the same reactions you use on posts.

## Who can message you

Choose who is allowed to start a conversation with you:

- **On the web:** **Settings → Privacy → Who can message you**
- **In the mobile app:** **Settings → Privacy → Messages from**

The choices are:

- **Everyone** (the default): anyone can message you. People who aren't your friends arrive as message requests (see below).
- **Friends only**: only your friends can start a conversation with you.
- **Nobody**: no one can start a new conversation with you.

The same setting applies to people on other servers.

## Message requests

When someone who isn't your friend messages you, the conversation arrives as a **message request** instead of going straight into your inbox. On the web, requests are listed separately under **Message requests**. In the mobile app, they're marked **Message request** in your conversation list.

You can read a request, but you can't reply until you accept it:

- **Accept** moves it into your conversations, and you can reply as normal.
- **Decline** (on the web) removes the conversation from your list. The sender isn't told.

Messages from friends never need accepting.

## Editing and deleting a message

You can change or remove any message you sent:

- **On the web:** hover over your message, open its menu, and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.
- **In the mobile app:** press and hold your message, then choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

An edited message is marked **edited**. A deleted message is replaced with **Message deleted** for both of you.

## Messaging people on other servers

You can message people on other Agora instances, and people on Mastodon and other [Fediverse](fediverse.md) servers, the same way you message someone on your own instance. Bluesky accounts can't be messaged from Agora, because Bluesky has its own separate messaging system.

A few things work differently when the other person is on another server:

- **Your messages are stored on both servers.** Messages on Agora are not end-to-end encrypted. In a conversation that crosses servers, the administrators of either server can read them. This is true of direct messages anywhere on the Fediverse, including Mastodon. A note at the top of the conversation reminds you when this applies.
- **Edits and deletions are sent to their server too.** How an edit or a deletion is shown there depends on the software that server runs.
- **Read status stays private.** Whether you've read a message is never sent to the other server.
- **Conversations are one-to-one.** Group conversations don't cross servers.
