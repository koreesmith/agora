# Getting Started with Agora

## Creating your account

1. Go to your Agora instance's home page and click **Get started — free** (or **Join the waitlist** if your instance uses invite-only mode).
2. Enter your desired **username**, **email address**, and a secure **password**.
3. Check your email for a verification link and click it to activate your account.
4. Once verified, sign in. The first time you do, a short getting started tour walks you through the basics (see below).

> **Tip:** Your username is permanent and public — choose something you're comfortable sharing.

## The getting started tour

The first time you sign in on the web, Agora walks you through a few short steps:

1. **Welcome:** what makes Agora different.
2. **Your profile:** a photo, display name, pronouns, and bio.
3. **Privacy:** who can see your posts and profile, and who can message you.
4. **Bluesky** and **Fediverse:** your handle on each network, and whether your public posts go there. Either one can be turned off. (A step is left out if your instance doesn't use that network.)
5. **Find people:** friends on your instance, people to follow on Bluesky or the Fediverse, and groups to join.
6. **All set:** a recap, and a map of where everything lives.

Every step is optional. **Skip setup** at the top leaves everything as it is, and anything you choose can be changed later in **Settings**. Either way, the tour won't show up on its own again.

To go through it again, click the **compass** icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
