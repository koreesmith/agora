# Commenting & Reacting

## Reacting to a post

Every post has a reaction button (a thumbs up) under it.

- **Tap or click** it to give the post a 👍 **Like**.
- **Press and hold** it to open the full set of reactions, then slide to the one you want and let go.

The reactions are:

| | |
|---|---|
| 👍 Like | 🙏 Thankful |
| ❤️ Love | 🏳️‍🌈 Pride |
| 😂 Laugh | 😢 Sad |
| 😮 Wow | 😡 Angry |
| 🫂 Care | 👎 Dislike |

You can have one reaction on a post at a time. Picking a different one replaces it, and picking the one you already chose removes it.

To see who reacted, click the row of reaction emoji under the post (on the web) or tap one of the reaction counts (in the mobile app). The list can be filtered by reaction.

Mastodon and Bluesky only have a plain like, with no named reactions. Likes from either network arrive as 👍 **Like**. When you react to a post from either network, Like, Love, Laugh, Wow, Care, Thankful and Pride are sent there as a like. Sad, Angry and Dislike have no equivalent, so they stay on Agora. People on other Agora servers see your actual reaction. See [The Fediverse](fediverse.md) and [Bluesky](bluesky.md).

## Writing a comment

- **On the web:** type in the **Write a comment…** box under a post and press Enter. Click the comment count to open the full conversation, where you can also attach an image with the **Add image** button.
- **In the mobile app:** type in the **Write a comment…** box under a post and tap send, or tap the comment button to open the post and its full conversation. The image button beside the comment box attaches a photo.

A comment can be text, an image, or both. Use **@username** to tag someone, the same as in a post. They'll be notified.

The post's author is notified when you comment. If you comment on a post from Mastodon or Bluesky, your comment is sent there as a reply.

## Replying to a comment

Choose **Reply** under a comment. The reply box starts with the person's @username so they're notified.

Conversations go up to three levels deep: a comment, a reply to it, and a reply to that reply. Replies at the third level don't have a **Reply** button.

## Reacting to a comment

Comments have their own reaction button. Tap or click it for 👍, or press and hold it for the full set. In the mobile app, you then tap the reaction you want.

## Editing and deleting a comment

Open the **⋯** menu on your comment:

- **Edit** (on the web) or **Edit comment** (in the mobile app) lets you change the text. An edited comment is marked **edited**.
- **Delete** (on the web) or **Delete comment** (in the mobile app) removes it. In the mobile app you're asked to confirm, because it can't be undone.

On the web, you can also delete comments other people leave on your own posts.

## Reporting a comment

If a comment breaks the rules, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Report** (on the web) or **Report comment** (in the mobile app). See [Reporting content](reporting.md).

## If you're not signed in

Anyone can read the comments on a public post, but you need to [sign in](getting-started.md) to comment or react.
