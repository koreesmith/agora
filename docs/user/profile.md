# Your Profile

Your profile is the page people see when they click or tap your name. It shows your photos, your details, and your posts.

## Editing your profile

- **On the web:** go to **Settings → Profile**.
- **In the mobile app:** open the **Profile** tab and tap **Edit profile**. Tap **Save** when you're done.

You can change:

- **Cover photo:** the wide banner at the top of your profile. On the web, use **Change** to upload a new one, then **Reposition** to drag it into place and **Save position**. In the mobile app, tap the banner (**Tap to add cover photo**) to pick one.
- **Avatar:** your profile picture. Use **Change avatar** on the web, or **Change photo** in the mobile app.
- **Display name:** the name shown on your posts and profile. This is separate from your @username, which can't be changed.
- **Pronouns:** shown in brackets beside your name on your profile and posts. Up to 50 characters.
- **Bio:** a few lines about yourself.
- **Location**
- **Website:** shown as a link on your profile.

On the web, click **Save profile** to save your changes. The cover photo and avatar save as soon as you upload them.

## What people see on your profile

At the top of your profile are your cover photo, avatar, display name, pronouns, @username, bio, number of friends, location and website. Below that are tabs:

- **Posts:** the posts you've written that the viewer is allowed to see.
- **Photos:** photos from your posts.
- **Wall:** posts your friends have written on your wall.

People who aren't signed in only see the **Posts** tab.

What appears in **Posts** depends on each post's own visibility. A friends-only post shows up only for your friends, for example. See [Privacy settings](privacy.md).

## Limiting who can see your profile

Two settings control your profile. On the web they're in **Settings → Privacy**. In the mobile app they're under **Privacy** in **Settings**, and also on the **Edit profile** screen.

- **Private profile:** only your friends can see your profile page and timeline. Everyone else sees **This profile is private** and a prompt to add you as a friend.
- **Hide timeline** (**Hide timeline from profile** on the web): nobody can browse your post history on your profile, not even friends. Your posts still appear in your friends' feeds as normal.

## Your wall

Your friends can write on your wall from your profile with **Write on wall**. Wall posts appear on the **Wall** tab of your profile.

To review wall posts before they appear, turn on **Approve wall posts** in your privacy settings. New wall posts then wait under **Pending approval** on your wall until you approve or reject them.

## Visiting someone else's profile

On a friend's profile you'll find:

- **Message:** start a [direct message](messages.md).
- **Notify me:** get a notification whenever they post. Click again (**Notifying**) to turn it off.
- **Write on wall:** post on their wall.
- **Friends:** you're friends. Click it to unfriend them.
- **Block:** see [Blocking someone](blocking.md).

On the profile of someone who isn't your friend yet, you can send a friend request. See [Adding friends](friends.md). Profiles of Fediverse and Bluesky accounts have **Follow** instead, and show **Follows you** if they follow you. See [The Fediverse](fediverse.md) and [Bluesky](bluesky.md).
